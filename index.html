Physical Oceanographer · Scientific Programmer · Data Scientist

What I Do

  • Reproducible workflows for geospatial and oceanographic data (buoys, gliders, moorings, altimetry).
  • QC and production of long-term datasets, including a 13-year Stratus mooring record.
  • Satellite + models: SWOT SSH data assimilation, coupled climate-model analysis.
  • AI-powered flood monitoring integrating NOAA alerts and real-time news.

Featured Work

  • Deep Ocean Temperature from the Stratus Ocean Reference Station (2012–2025) — data descriptor in preparation, to be submitted to Nature Scientific Data.
  • Python-based data assimilation methods improving SWOT SSH accuracy (UC San Diego postdoc).
  • Southern Ocean mesoscale studies: mixed-layer heat budgets, air–sea coupling, mixed-layer depth variability (Ph.D., University of Miami).
